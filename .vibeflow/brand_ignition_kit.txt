### **Section 1: Mission & Vision (Your Purpose & Your Future)**

- If your company was a person, what would be its core purpose in life?
- Imagine your company shuts down today. What specific void is left in the market or the world? What problem now goes unsolved?
- Forget your product for a moment. What is the one fundamental change you are trying to make for your customers?
- Now, let's look 10 years into the future. Your company has been wildly successful beyond your dreams. Describe the world you have helped create. What does it look like? How is it different?

### **Section 2: Values (Your Guiding Principles)**

- Describe a difficult decision the company had to make in the last year. What principles guided you to the final choice?
- What behavior gets someone promoted or celebrated within your organization, even if it doesn't show up on a performance review?
- Conversely, what action, attitude, or behavior would absolutely not be tolerated here, no matter how skilled the person was?
- If you had to describe your company's "code of conduct" in three simple rules, what would they be?

### **Section 3: The Audience (Their Core Motivations)**

- **The Core Driver:** Forget demographics. Think about the internal monologue of your ideal customer. What is the core motivation or deep-seated desire that drives them in their professional or personal life? What outcome are they ultimately trying to achieve?
- **The Job-to-be-Done:** Customers "hire" a product to do a specific "job." (e.g., They don't buy a drill; they buy a hole in the wall). What functional job are your customers hiring you for? More importantly, what emotional "job" is being done? (e.g., Do they feel more competent, secure, innovative, or relieved after using your product?)
- **Pains & The "Stupid Tax":** Describe the most frustrating, inefficient, or costly part of their current process that your product eliminates. What "stupid tax" are they paying by sticking with the status quo?
- **Watering Holes:** Where do these people congregate to learn and share ideas? Be specific about the newsletters, podcasts, subreddits, influencers, or conferences they trust.
- **What are the top 1-2 reasons your ideal customer would hesitate or choose *not* to use a solution like yours? What are their biggest fears or perceived risks?**

### **Section 4: Positioning & Value (Your Place in the World)**

- Who or what is the "enemy"? This could be a competitor, an outdated technology, a frustrating process, or a flawed ideology that your brand stands against.
- What is something you believe to be true about your market that most other people in it would disagree with?
- List 3-4 things your product or service allows your customers to do. For each one, answer: "So what?" until you get to a core human or business benefit.
- **What are your 'table stakes'—the features customers absolutely expect in your category? Now, what are your 3 undeniable 'proof points' that make your unique claim credible? (e.g., a specific data point, a key partnership, a proprietary technology, a customer success story).**

### **Section 5: Emotion & Personality (Your Soul)**

- If your brand was a feeling, what would it be? (e.g., Relief, Confidence, Exhilaration, Safety).
- Describe your brand's personality in three adjectives (e.g., "Bold, Witty, and Authoritative").
- **To add nuance, for each adjective, provide a 'We are X, but not Y' statement. (e.g., 'We are authoritative, but not arrogant.' 'We are witty, but not silly.')**
