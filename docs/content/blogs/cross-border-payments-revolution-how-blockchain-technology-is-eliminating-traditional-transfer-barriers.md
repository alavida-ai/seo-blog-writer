## The Hidden Costs Crushing Traditional Cross-Border Payments

### The Real Price Beyond Exchange Rates

- **Transfer fees**: $15-25 upfront
- **Correspondent banking charges**: $10-30 in hidden intermediary fees
- **Exchange rate markups**: 2-4% above mid-market rates ($20-40 on a $1,000 transfer)
- **Receiving bank fees**: $5-15 charged to your recipient

Your $1,000 transfer actually costs $62 in total fees, while your recipient receives only $938. Meanwhile, blockchain-powered cross-border payment solutions like Send.it complete the same transfer for under $5 total cost.

## Why Blockchain Transforms International Money Transfers

### Eliminating Intermediaries Through Distributed Networks

Traditional cross-border payments require a complex web of correspondent banks, clearinghouses, and regulatory intermediaries. A typical SWIFT transfer from New York to Mumbai routes through:

1. Originating bank (Chase)
2. Correspondent bank in New York (JPMorgan)
3. Intermediary bank in London (HSBC)
4. Correspondent bank in Mumbai (Standard Chartered)
5. Receiving bank (ICICI)

Each intermediary adds fees, processing time, and potential failure points. Blockchain-based global payment systems eliminate this complexity by creating direct peer-to-peer settlement networks.

When you send money through Send.it's blockchain infrastructure:
1. Your payment is validated by the distributed network
2. Smart contracts execute the currency conversion
3. Funds are instantly settled in the recipient's account

This reduction from 5-7 intermediaries to direct settlement cuts costs by 40-60% while eliminating the 2-5 business day delays inherent in correspondent banking.

### 24/7 Settlement Networks Replace Banking Hours

Traditional banks operate on business day schedules, creating artificial delays for urgent transfers. Send money on Friday evening, and your recipient waits until Tuesday for settlement due to weekend and holiday closures.

Blockchain networks operate continuously, processing transactions every few seconds regardless of time zones or holidays. JPMorgan's JPM Coin has already processed over $300 billion in institutional transactions, demonstrating that major financial institutions recognize blockchain's settlement advantages.

Send.it leverages this always-on infrastructure to provide instant settlement for cross-border payments, turning what was once a multi-day ordeal into a real-time experience comparable to domestic digital payments.
